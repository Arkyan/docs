TrucklineMP offers optional **Supporter** and **Premium** subscriptions. They help fund the platform and unlock cosmetic perks on your profile - no gameplay or moderation advantages.

## Accessing Billing

1. Click your avatar in the top-right corner
2. Select **Settings**
3. Select the **Billing** tab
4. Or navigate to `trucklinemp.com/user/me/settings?tab=billing`

---

## Subscribing

1. Choose **Supporter** or **Premium**
2. Click **Subscribe** - you're redirected to a secure Stripe checkout
3. Complete payment
4. You're returned to Billing settings with your subscription active

Manage or cancel your subscription anytime from the **Manage Billing** button, which opens the Stripe customer portal.

### Linking Patreon instead

If you already support TrucklineMP on Patreon, you can link your Patreon account from [Connections](/web-docs/account/connections/) instead of subscribing directly - your tier is detected automatically. This requires Patreon linking to be enabled on the platform.

## Troubleshooting

### "My checkout didn't complete"

If payment fails or is cancelled, no subscription is created - try again from the Billing tab.

### "My perks aren't showing"

Perks apply automatically once your subscription is active. Refresh your profile page; if it's still missing after a few minutes, check that your subscription status is **Active** in Billing settings.

### "I linked Patreon but don't have perks"

Confirm your Patreon pledge tier matches a supported tier, and that the connection shows as linked under [Connections](/web-docs/account/connections/).
