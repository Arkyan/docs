New accounts get a short, guided walkthrough of the platform led by **John Driver**, a character who introduces the essentials one step at a time.

## What it covers

The tour walks through the platform in stages, including:

- **Dashboard** — your home base for recent activity and quick access
- **Profile** — setting your handle, colors, and details
- **Appearance** — adjusting theme and colors to your liking
- **VTCs** — joining or starting a Virtual Trucking Company
- **Events** — the convoy and community-drive board
- **News** — platform-wide updates
- **Polls** — community votes
- **Notifications** — where replies, mentions, and alerts collect

Each step is a short dialogue with John Driver, with a **Continue** button to move forward.

---

## Skipping the tour

Click **Skip** at any point. You'll be asked to confirm — the tour can always be restarted later, so skipping isn't permanent.

---

## Restarting the tour

If you want to go through it again:

1. Open **Settings** → **Appearance**
2. Scroll to the **Onboarding** section
3. Click **Restart guided tour**

See [Appearance & Preferences](/guides/account/appearance-preferences/) for the full settings reference.
