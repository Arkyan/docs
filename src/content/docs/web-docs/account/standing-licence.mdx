Your account standing reflects your moderation history on TrucklineMP. Your driver licence is a separate record that tracks driving-related demerits. Both are shown on your account pages and on your public profile where policy allows.

## Account standing

Open [trucklinemp.com/account/standing](https://trucklinemp.com/account/standing) while signed in to see your current standing.

Standing tiers: all good, limited, very limited, at risk, suspended. Your standing page explains what your tier means and what restrictions apply.

VTC bans are tracked under the same standing page at `/account/standing/vtc-bans`. This shows bans issued against you in companies you belong to.

## Driver licence

Open [trucklinemp.com/account/licence](https://trucklinemp.com/account/licence) to see your driver licence. The page shows your current tier, demerit score, and active incidents with their reason, points, and clearing date.

Your public profile shows a licence card with the tier only. The full demerit balance and incident history are private to you.

## Appeals

If you disagree with a ban or suspension, use the appeal flow in [Bans & Appeals](/web-docs/account/bans-appeals/). The system checks eligibility when you start an appeal and explains when you can try again if you are not eligible.
