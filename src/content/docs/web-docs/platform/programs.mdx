TrucklineMP runs community programs that recognize players and companies for their contributions. These include the Bug Hunters program, the Contributors program, and the Recognition awards.

## Programs

Open [trucklinemp.com/programs](https://trucklinemp.com/programs) to see the active programs and their details.

Each program page explains what the program is, how to take part, and what recognition is available.

## Bug Hunters

The Bug Hunters program rewards players who find and report bugs through the support system. Bug reports are reviewed by the team and verified bugs earn recognition.

## Contributors

The Contributors program recognizes people who contribute to the platform or its documentation. Contributors appear on the [Contributors](/web-docs/contribute/contributors/) page and may receive badges.

## Recognition

Recognition awards celebrate notable contributions to the community. Recognition is assigned by the team and appears on your public profile.
