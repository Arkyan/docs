Community polls let the community vote on platform questions, event ideas, and other topics. Open [trucklinemp.com/polls](https://trucklinemp.com/polls) to see open polls.

## Voting

Voting requires a signed-in account. Each poll shows its question, options, close time, and whether results are public.

Some polls are restricted to supporters. Supporter polls are marked on the poll page.

## Results

Results open after the poll closes, or earlier if the poll allows it. Poll results are shared according to the poll settings.
