TrucklineMP publishes official news about the platform, events, and community updates. News is public and does not require an account to read.

## Reading news

Open [trucklinemp.com/news](https://trucklinemp.com/news) to browse the latest posts. Posts are grouped by category such as General, Platform, Community, Development, or Events, and can be filtered from the top of the page.

## Individual posts

Open any post to read the full article. Posts support rich formatting, images, and links. Pinned posts stay at the top of the feed until they are unpinned.

## Reactions

Signed-in users can react to news posts. Reactions are a quick way to show support or feedback on an article.
