Partnerships connect your company with other VTCs or VBCs. Partners appear on your public profile as a partner preview, which cross-promotes both companies.

Partnerships are separate from [Sister Companies](/web-docs/vtc/sister-companies/) (which link a VTC to a VBC with shared staff) and from the [Partnered VTC Program](/web-docs/vtc-programs/partnered/) (a platform recognition program).

## Accessing partnerships

Open your company Manage panel and select **Partnerships** from the sidebar (`/vtc/@handle/manage/partnerships`).

## Sending a request

1. Search for the company you want to partner with by name or handle.
2. Select the company from the results.
3. Optionally write a short message to the owner.
4. Send the request.

The target company owner reviews the request in their own Partnerships page and can accept or decline it.

## Managing requests

The Partnerships page shows three lists:

| List | What it contains |
|------|------------------|
| Incoming | Requests other companies sent to you, waiting for your decision |
| Outgoing | Requests you sent that have not been answered yet |
| Partners | Companies you are currently partnered with |

Accept a request to add the company to your partner list. Decline or remove a partner at any time to end the partnership.
