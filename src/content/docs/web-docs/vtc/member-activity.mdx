The Member Activity page in your company management panel shows how active each member is and lets staff manage inactivity policy and leave requests. Open it from the sidebar (`/vtc/@handle/manage/member-activity`).

Access requires the `management.members.view_activity` permission. Owners always have access.

## Activity statuses

Each member has an activity status based on their recent participation:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Active | Participated recently |
| At risk | Inactive for a while and close to the inactivity threshold |
| Inactive | Inactive for longer than the allowed period |
| On leave | Covered by an approved leave request |
| Exempt | Exempt from activity requirements |

The page shows days since each member was last active and how many events they joined in the review period.

## Inactivity policy

Staff can set the maximum number of inactive days allowed before a member is marked inactive. Members marked inactive can be removed from the company or given a leave request to protect their membership.

## Leave requests

Members can request leave if they cannot participate for a while. Staff review pending requests and approve or reject them. Approved leave protects the member from the inactivity policy until the leave ends.
