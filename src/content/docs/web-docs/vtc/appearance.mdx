The Appearance page in your company management panel controls how your public company page looks. It has two parts: the theme editor and the home layout builder.

Open **Appearance** from the sidebar (`/vtc/@handle/manage/appearance`). Requires the `management.vtc.appearance` permission. Owners always have access.

## Theme editor

The theme editor sets the accent colors, background colors, and text colors used across your company page. Changes preview live before you save them.

Advanced theme options are locked for most companies. They unlock for companies that hold both Verified and Partnered status.

Colors must pass readability contrast checks before they can be saved so that text stays legible on the page.

## Home layout builder

The home layout builder rearranges the sections shown on your public company page. You can add, move, resize, or remove sections such as the overview, rules, members, events, news, gallery, or recruitment.

The layout is only available to companies that can access custom page layout. See the [Verified VTC Program](/web-docs/vtc-programs/verified/) and [Partnered VTC Program](/web-docs/vtc-programs/partnered/) for eligibility.
