The **Analytics** tab in your management panel gives staff a quick read on how your company is trending over the last 90 days - no spreadsheet exports required.

## Accessing Analytics

1. Open your VTC or VBC **Manage** panel
2. Select **Analytics** from the sidebar
3. Or navigate directly to `/vtc/@handle/manage/analytics` (or `/vbc/@handle/manage/analytics`)

The tab only appears for staff whose role holds the `analytics.view` permission. See [Roles & Permissions](/web-docs/vtc/roles-permissions/) to grant it.

---

## What's shown

All charts cover a rolling **90-day window**.

### Member Growth

Members joined and members who left, per day. Useful for spotting recruitment pushes or unusual churn.

### Applications

Two figures tracked separately by the date they actually happened:

- **Submitted** - counted on the day the application was filed
- **Accepted / Rejected** - counted on the day staff reviewed it, not the day it was submitted

This means an application submitted on one day and accepted a few days later shows up correctly on both dates, instead of bunching everything on the submission day.

### Event Attendance

Members marked as **Going** for each event held in the window, so you can compare turnout across events.

### News Engagement

Reactions and comments on VTC News posts, per day - a quick signal for which announcements actually land with members.

---

## Permissions

Analytics has its own permission slug, separate from general management access:

| Permission | What it grants |
|------------|-----------------|
| `analytics.view` | View the Analytics tab and its charts |

Grant it to any role that should see company-level metrics - for example Management or a dedicated Staff role - from the **Permissions** tab in [Roles & Permissions](/web-docs/vtc/roles-permissions/).

---

## Troubleshooting

### "I don't see an Analytics tab"

Your role doesn't have the `analytics.view` permission yet. Ask an owner or admin to grant it.

### "A chart looks empty"

Charts only render once there's at least one data point in the last 90 days for that metric (for example, no applications submitted yet means an empty Applications chart).
