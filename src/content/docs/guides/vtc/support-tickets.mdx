VTC Support gives your members a direct line to staff — separate from [TrucklineMP's own support system](/guides/account/support-tickets/), which is for platform-level issues.

## For members: submitting a ticket

1. Go to your VTC or VBC's profile
2. Open `/vtc/@handle/support` (or `/vbc/@handle/support`)
3. Pick a category, write a subject and message, and submit

Staff are notified and can reply directly in the ticket thread.

---

## For staff: managing tickets

1. Open your **Manage** panel
2. Select **Support**

### Tickets tab

- Filter by status (**Open**, **In Progress**, **Waiting on User**, **Resolved**, **Closed**), category, priority, assignment, or search
- Claim unassigned tickets or filter to **Assigned to me**
- Reply, change status, and set priority from the ticket thread

At-a-glance metrics show open tickets, tickets waiting on the user, tickets assigned to you, unassigned tickets, and average satisfaction rating.

### Settings tab

Staff with the **Support Settings** permission can configure:

- **Categories** — custom ticket categories with colors
- **Canned reply templates** — reusable responses for common questions

## Permissions

| Permission | What it grants |
|------------|-----------------|
| `support.view` | View tickets |
| `support.reply` | Reply to tickets |
| `support.assign` | Assign tickets to staff |
| `support.close` | Resolve and close tickets |
| `support.manage` | Full ticket workflow control |
| `support.settings` | Configure categories and templates |

Assign these from the **Permissions** tab in [Roles & Permissions](/guides/vtc/roles-permissions/). Support must also be enabled for your company before the tab appears to members.

---

## Troubleshooting

### "Members can't find the support page"

Confirm VTC Support is enabled for your company, and share the direct link: `/vtc/@handle/support`.

### "I can't see the Support tab in Manage"

You need at least `support.view` (or `management.vtc.edit`, which always grants access).

### "I can't edit categories or templates"

Editing configuration requires `support.settings`, which is separate from ticket-handling permissions.
