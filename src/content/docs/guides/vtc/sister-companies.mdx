Sister Companies are partnerships on TrucklineMP that link a Virtual Trucking Company (VTC) and a Virtual Bus Company (VBC) together. Linking sister companies helps cross-advertise organizations, coordinate events, and optionally share staff members between trucking and coach divisions.

---

## Prerequisites

Before linking, ensure that:
1. **Opposite Types**: You can only link a VTC (trucking company) with a VBC (coach/bus company). You cannot link two VTCs or two VBCs.
2. **Ownership**: You must be the owner of the company initiating the request, and the target company's owner must accept the request.
3. **No Existing Link**: Neither company can have an active sister company. A company can only have one sister company at a time.

---

## Linking Companies

To link with a sister company:

1. Navigate to your company profile.
2. Click **Manage** to open the Management Console.
3. Select the **Links** tab on the sidebar.
4. Under **Send Link Request**, enter the unique handle of the target company.
5. (Optional) Write a short message to the owner of the target company.
6. (Optional) Check **Request Shared Staff** if you want members to hold ranks in both companies.
7. Click **Send Request**.

The target company's owner will receive a notification to review, accept, or decline the request in their own management console under **Links**.

---

## Managing Shared Staff

When sending or accepting a link request, you can configure how member accounts interact across both organizations:

### Request Shared Staff
If **Request Shared Staff** is enabled, it allows drivers or coach operators to hold memberships and ranks in both sister companies simultaneously. This is ideal for organizations that share a community but operate separate trucking and coach divisions.

### External Membership Policy
Within the **Links** management page, owners can set their **External Membership Policy**:
- **Allowed**: Members can freely join other companies outside of the sister-company relationship.
- **Blocked**: Members are restricted from joining any other company unless it is the linked sister company. If they attempt to join another organization, they will be blocked or prompted to leave.

---

## Unlinking

If you wish to terminate the sister company relationship:
1. Navigate to the **Links** management page.
2. Under the active link status, click the **Unlink** button.
3. Confirm the action in the popup dialog.

Unlinking is instant. Once unlinked, the sister company relationship is severed, and any cross-company shared staff permissions are revoked according to the companies' individual membership rules.
